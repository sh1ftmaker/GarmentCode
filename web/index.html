Fabric

How the cloth behaves and looks.

Stretch by direction

The warp runs along the selvedge and is the firmest, the weft runs across and gives a little more; on the bias (45°) a woven shears, so bias-cut pieces stretch, cling and drape softly. Knits stretch mostly crosswise. Choose the grain per garment in Design, per piece in Cutting.

Cutting layout

Your pattern pieces, with seam allowance, arranged on a bolt of fabric at real size.

Lay the fabric

Folded: selvedges together, right sides in. Mirrored pairs are cut through both layers at once and symmetric pieces are cut on the fold.

Drag pieces to adjust; tap one to turn it 180°. 1 cm is kept free along each selvedge.

      About this studio

      GarmentCode 3D Studio is an unofficial, community-built web front-end for GarmentCode, the parametric sewing-pattern system by Maria Korosteleva and Olga Sorkine-Hornung (ETH Zürich). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the GarmentCode authors.

      • Patterns are drafted by GarmentCode's original Python code, running in your browser through Pyodide.
      • FreeSewing designs: the whole FreeSewing collection (72 designs by Joost De Cock and many other designers; each design names its designers in the picker) is drafted by FreeSewing's own code (MIT, by Joost De Cock and contributors) from your measurements. Every design gets its pattern, cutting layout and exports; the 54 marked 3D (all the garments except Diana, Tina, Breanna and Cornelius; accessories, toys and costumes are pattern-only) are also converted into panels for the drape. Not affiliated with FreeSewing either.
      • The mannequin is a measurement-driven morph of GarmentCode's base bodies.
      • The drape is a lightweight real-time cloth simulation modelled on GarmentCode's simulation setup. It isn't the NVIDIA Warp simulator used for the GarmentCodeData dataset.
      • Nothing leaves your device. Designs are saved in your browser.

      If you use GarmentCode in research, please cite the original papers (see the repository README).
